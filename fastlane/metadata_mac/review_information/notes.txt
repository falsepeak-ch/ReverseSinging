No account is needed. Everything runs on device; nothing is uploaded. Firebase provides anonymous usage statistics and crash reports only, never audio or file contents.

MOVIE SCENE DUB

The app ships with four short example scenes and does not host, provide or distribute any other film, clip or dialogue: no catalogue, no search, no download. Every further scene is a file the user already has and imports.

1. Three are from Blender's open movies under Creative Commons Attribution, which permits adaptation and redistribution with credit: "Camp Rules" (23s, "Sprite Fright", CC BY 4.0), "Awesome in Space" (23s, "Tears of Steel", CC BY 3.0) and "A Kindred Spirit" (29s, "Sintel", CC BY 3.0).

2. "Stuck Up" (35s), from "The Outsider" (Centron Productions, 1951), a US classroom film never renewed in its 28th year, so US public domain; Copyright Office post-1977 records show no renewal.

Each states its provenance (film, year, producer, terms, links) on the scene screen and before every export.

THE PURCHASE

The Mac app shares the iPhone app's record and in-app purchases (universal purchase). Free to download, with no trial. Reverse Singing and Home Video Dub are free for everyone. Movie Scene Dub and Sound Imitation stay locked until a purchase, which a sandbox account can make. A review device meets the locked games on first launch.

Any of three products unlocks the same content: the non-consumable "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.lifetime", or the auto-renewable subscriptions "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.monthly" and "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.yearly" (7-day free trial on the yearly). The purchase screen shows either the lifetime product alone or the two subscriptions, chosen per install by a remote test. Users who installed before the paywall existed keep the paid games free for life.

To reach the purchase screen: pick a locked game in the sidebar, or the button at the foot of the sidebar, or Dubloon > Settings > Dubloon Pro.

BOOTH CAM

Booth Cam films the performer with the Mac's camera while they dub. Off by default, it never starts on its own: the camera button shows the app's own explanation first, then the system prompt, and declining costs nothing. Clips never leave the device and appear only in a video the user exports.

TWO NOTICES, SHOWN EVERY TIME

Before any import, the app asks whether the user already has the movie files. If not, a panel states, before any link, that the destination is an unaffiliated third-party site, that we host no files, and that the user is responsible for the rights to what they download.

Before any export, the app states that the video contains someone else's work; names that work and its terms; asks that the credit stay with the video; and says that sharing it is the user's responsibility, within local law. For a scene the user imported, it says instead that the app cannot know its origin or terms.

HOW TO TEST

In the sidebar, open "Movie Scene Dub" and pick a bundled scene. Press R (or the red button under the picture) to record the selected line, then choose "My Dub" above the picture, or File > Export Video. The source and licence sit at the bottom of the inspector on the right. For the ownership gate, choose File > Import Dub Pack; it appears every time. For Booth Cam, use the camera button in the toolbar.

SOUND IMITATION

Plays one of the bundled sound effects, all CC0 from Freesound (credited per sound), and scores the user's imitation on the device. A video is made only when the user asks for one.

HOME VIDEO DUB

The user picks their own video from the system Photos picker or from Finder (Open panel or drag and drop), records a voice over it and may export it. Nothing is uploaded. To test, open "Home Video Dub" in the sidebar.

MICROPHONE

All four games record the user's voice. The first press of Record shows the app's explanation with one button, which always opens the system prompt. Nothing follows a refusal. Recordings never leave the device.
