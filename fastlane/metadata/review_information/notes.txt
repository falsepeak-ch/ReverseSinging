No account is needed. Everything runs on device; nothing is uploaded. Firebase provides anonymous usage statistics and crash reports only, never audio or file contents.

MOVIE SCENE DUB

The app ships with two short example scenes and does not host, provide or distribute any other film, clip or dialogue: no catalogue, no search, no download. Every further scene is a file the user already has and imports.

1. "Camp Rules" (23s), from "Sprite Fright" (Blender Studio, 2021), released by its rights holder under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, which permits adaptation and redistribution on the single condition of credit.

2. "Stuck Up" (35s), from "The Outsider" (Centron Productions, 1951), a US classroom film whose copyright was never renewed in its 28th year and is therefore US public domain. The Copyright Office's post-1977 records show no renewal.

Both scenes state their provenance (film, year, producer, terms, with links) on the scene screen and before every export.

THE PURCHASE

Free to download, with no trial. Reverse Singing and Home Video Dub are free for everyone. Movie Scene Dub and Sound Imitation stay locked until a purchase, which a sandbox account can make. Sandbox download dates are ignored, so a review device meets the paywall.

Any of three products unlocks the same content: the non-consumable "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.lifetime", or the auto-renewable subscriptions "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.monthly" and "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.yearly" (7-day free trial on the yearly). The purchase screen shows either the lifetime product alone or the two subscriptions, chosen per install by a remote test; both are the real, complete offer. Users who installed before the paywall existed keep the paid games free for life.

To reach the purchase screen: tap a locked game, or the "Get Dubloon Pro to play" card on the main menu, or the sliders icon at the top right, then "Unlock Dubloon".

BOOTH CAM

Booth Cam films the performer with the front camera while they dub. Off by default, it never starts on its own: tapping the camera key shows the app's own explanation first, then the system prompt, and declining costs nothing. Clips never leave the device and appear only in a video the user exports.

TWO NOTICES, SHOWN EVERY TIME

Before any import, the app asks whether the user already has the movie files. If not, a panel states, before any link is offered, that the destination is an unaffiliated third-party site, that we neither host nor distribute files, and that the user is responsible for the rights to what they download and for obeying local copyright law.

Before any export, the app states that the video contains someone else's work; names that work and its terms; asks that the credit stay with the video; and says that sharing it is the user's responsibility, within local law. For a scene the user imported, it says instead that the app cannot know its origin or terms.

HOW TO TEST

Tap "Movie Scene Dub". The two bundled scenes install on first open: pick one, tap "Start Dubbing", record a line, then "Play My Dub" or "Export Video". The source and licence panel sits at the bottom of a scene's screen. For the ownership gate, tap "+" in the library; it appears every time. For Booth Cam, tap the camera key at the top right of the record screen; the simulator has no front camera.

New in 1.8.0: the library's "I need help getting more packs" row explains importing and repeats both disclaimers.

SOUND IMITATION

Plays one of the bundled sound effects, all CC0 from Freesound (credited per sound), and scores the user's imitation on the device.

HOME VIDEO DUB (NEW IN 1.8.0)

The user picks their own video with the system Photos picker (no library permission), records a voice over it and may export it. Nothing is uploaded.

MICROPHONE

All four games record the user's voice; the app explains this on its own onboarding screen before the system prompt. Recordings never leave the device.
