No account is needed. Everything runs on device; nothing is uploaded. Firebase provides anonymous usage statistics and crash reports only, never audio or file contents.

MOVIE SCENE DUB

The app ships four short scenes and hosts no other film, clip or dialogue. Every further scene is a file the user imports.

1. Three are from Blender's open movies under Creative Commons Attribution: "Camp Rules" ("Sprite Fright", CC BY 4.0), "Awesome in Space" ("Tears of Steel", CC BY 3.0) and "A Kindred Spirit" ("Sintel", CC BY 3.0).

2. "Stuck Up", from "The Outsider" (Centron Productions, 1951), a US classroom film never renewed, so US public domain.

Each states its provenance on the scene screen and before every export.

THE PURCHASE

Free to download. All four games open for everyone. The first 7 takes are free, counted across all games. After the seventh the purchase screen opens, and each new take asks for a purchase (sandbox works).

Any of three products unlocks the same content: the non-consumable "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.lifetime", or the auto-renewable subscriptions "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.monthly" and "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.yearly" (7-day free trial on the yearly). The purchase screen shows either the lifetime product alone or the two subscriptions, chosen per install by a remote test.

To reach the purchase screen: tap "Get Dubloon Pro" on the last onboarding page (skippable), a take after the free ones, the "Get Dubloon Pro to dub" card on the main menu, or the sliders icon, then "Unlock Dubloon".

BOOTH CAM

Booth Cam films the performer with the front camera. Off by default: tapping the camera key shows the app's own explanation first, then the system prompt, and declining costs nothing. Clips never leave the device and appear only in a video the user exports.

TWO NOTICES, SHOWN EVERY TIME

Before any import, the app asks whether the user already has the movie files. If not, a panel states, before any link is offered, that the destination is an unaffiliated third-party site, that we neither host nor distribute files, and that the user is responsible for the rights to what they download and for obeying local copyright law.

Before any export, the app states that the video contains someone else's work, names it and its terms, asks that the credit stay with it, and says sharing it is the user's responsibility. For a scene the user imported, it says instead that the app cannot know its origin or terms.

HOW TO TEST

Tap "Movie Scene Dub". The four bundled scenes install on first open: pick one, tap "Start Dubbing", record a line, then "Play My Dub" or "Export Video". The source and licence panel sits at the bottom of a scene's screen. For the ownership gate, tap "+" in the library; it appears every time.

The library's "I need help getting more packs" row explains importing and repeats both disclaimers.

HALLOWEEN (20 OCTOBER TO 2 NOVEMBER 2026)

A banner over the games offers "com.falsepeak.reverse_singing_ios.yearly_halloween": same access as the yearly plan, first year at a 2.99 USD pay-up-front introductory offer, then the normal yearly price. It shows its last day as a date, not a countdown. Tapping it opens a terms card (plan, both prices, auto-renewal, how to cancel, Terms and Privacy links); only Continue there opens the App Store sheet. Movie Scene Dub also lists a few horror scenes shared on GameBanana; each opens that page in Safari, under the same third-party and rights notice.

SOUND IMITATION

Plays a bundled sound (CC0, Freesound, credited per sound) and scores the user's imitation on the device.

HOME VIDEO DUB

The user picks their own video with the system Photos picker, records a voice over it and may export it. Nothing is uploaded.

MICROPHONE

Onboarding asks for nothing. The first press of Record shows the app's explanation with one button, which always opens the system prompt. Nothing follows a refusal. Recordings never leave the device.
