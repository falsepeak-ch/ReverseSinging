DIFFERENT APP, DIFFERENT AUDIENCE

Dubloon the Game is a variant of the original Dubloon app (Dubloon by Cluso, com.falsepeak.reverse-singing-ios), built for a different public. The original Dubloon is a voice-training tool for people who work with their voice: it is about sound training for professionals and serious practice (reverse singing, a full waveform editor with loop, speed and pitch, dubbing with Booth Cam, home-video narration and exports). Dubloon the Game is a gamified, casual version of the dubbing and sound-imitation exercises for players who want a quick, fun game: a host-led game show with stars, sound effects, Game Center leaderboards and achievements, and a weekly race. It has none of the professional tools. The two apps target different people and have separate bundles, purchases and Game Center.

No account is needed. Everything runs on device; nothing is uploaded. Firebase provides anonymous usage statistics and crash reports only, never audio or file contents. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device; nothing is sent from a server.

THE PURCHASE

Free to download, with no account. Both games open for everyone. The first 3 takes are free, counted across both games. After the third, the purchase screen opens, and each new take asks for a purchase, which a sandbox account can make. The gold button on the home stage also opens it.

Any of three products unlocks the same thing (unlimited takes): the non-consumable "com.falsepeak.dubloon_game.lifetime", or the auto-renewable subscriptions "com.falsepeak.dubloon_game.monthly" and "com.falsepeak.dubloon_game.yearly" (3-day free trial on the yearly). The purchase screen has Restore, Terms and Privacy links.

MICROPHONE

The microphone is asked for at the first press of the record button, after a one-button explanation that always leads to the system prompt. Nothing follows a refusal.

GAME CENTER

The player is signed in at launch. Stars and points go to five leaderboards (one weekly, recurring) and nine achievements; the trophy on the home stage opens them.
