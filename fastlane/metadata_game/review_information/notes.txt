HOW THIS DIFFERS FROM DUBLOON

Same developer as Dubloon (com.falsepeak.reverse-singing-ios), and both apps share a voice engine, so here is the difference plainly. Dubloon is a four-mode voice studio for iPhone and Mac: reverse singing with a waveform editor (loop, speed, pitch), Movie Scene Dub with a line-by-line editor, Sound Imitation, Home Video Dub over the user's own videos, and Booth Cam exports. Dubloon the Game is a separate, iPhone-only game show built around a host: short rounds she introduces and reacts to, stars per take, Game Center with five leaderboards (one a weekly race) and nine achievements, sound effects and evening reminders. It has two rounds (dub a scene, imitate a sound) and none of the studio: no reverse singing, no Home Video Dub, no waveform editor, no Mac app. Separate bundle, purchases and Game Center.

No account is needed. Everything runs on device; nothing is uploaded. Firebase provides anonymous usage statistics and crash reports only, never audio or file contents. Reminders are local notifications; nothing is sent from a server.

MOVIE SCENE DUB

The app ships four short scenes and hosts no other film, clip or dialogue: no catalogue, no search, no in-app download.

1. Three from Blender's open movies under Creative Commons Attribution: "Camp Rules" ("Sprite Fright", CC BY 4.0), "Awesome in Space" ("Tears of Steel", CC BY 3.0), "A Kindred Spirit" ("Sintel", CC BY 3.0).
2. "Stuck Up", from "The Outsider" (Centron, 1951), a US classroom film never renewed, so US public domain.

Each states its provenance on the scene screen and before every export.

More scenes are files the user imports. Before an import the app asks whether the user has scenes; someone who has none can open GameBanana (gamebanana.com), a community site where people share dub packs, in Safari, with a short guide to downloading a pack there and importing it with Files. The app says the user must have the right to use what they import; it never fetches or plays anything from that site itself.

THE PURCHASE

Free to download. Both rounds open for everyone. The first 7 takes are free, counted across both. After the seventh, the purchase screen opens, and each new take asks for a purchase, which a sandbox account can make. The gold button on the home stage also opens it.

Three products unlock the same thing (unlimited takes): the non-consumable "com.falsepeak.dubloon_game.lifetime" and the auto-renewable "com.falsepeak.dubloon_game.monthly" and "com.falsepeak.dubloon_game.yearly" (3-day free trial on the yearly). The purchase screen shows price, period and renewal terms, with Restore, Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links.

HALLOWEEN DEAL (20 OCTOBER TO 2 NOVEMBER 2026)

While the deal runs, a banner over the home stage offers "com.falsepeak.dubloon_game.yearly_halloween": the same Pro access as the yearly plan, its first year at a 2.99 USD pay-up-front introductory offer, then the normal yearly price. The banner shows its last day as a date, not a countdown. Tapping it opens a terms card first: plan name, both prices, auto-renewal and how to cancel, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links. Only Continue there opens the App Store sheet. The regular purchase screen keeps the regular plans.

MICROPHONE

Asked at the first press of record, after a one-button explanation that always leads to the system prompt. Nothing follows a refusal.

GAME CENTER

Authentication starts at launch without blocking play. A player who is not signed in sees Game Center's sign-in only after tapping the trophy, which also opens the leaderboards and achievements.
