# Project notes for Claude

## Naming

- **Cluso is a former name we no longer own.** Keep "cluso" in the App Store keywords of our apps
  (it still brings in people searching for the old name), but never put it anywhere visible: no
  app or store name, subtitle, description, promotional text, release notes, screenshots, website
  copy or in-app text, in any locale or any of our apps (Dubloon, Dubloon for Mac, Dubloon the
  Game). App Review rejected Dubloon 1.11.0 under guideline 4.1(a) for "Dubloon by Cluso".
- The Dubloon store name is "Dubloon - Dub Movie Scenes", localized per locale as "Dubloon - "
  plus that locale's words for dubbing film scenes.
- Internal identifiers that still contain "cluso" (e.g. the RevenueCat entitlement
  `reverso_by_cluso_pro`) are never shown to users and stay as they are.
