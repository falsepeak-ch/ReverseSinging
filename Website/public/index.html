Pick one from the menu. Neither will make you sound normal.

Dubloon Pro

Movie Scene Dub

Load a scene, hear the original line, then record your own. The original waveform sits under yours, so you see exactly where you came in early or ran long.

  • LINELine by line, at your own pace, every character colour-coded
  • PACEA pacing bar that turns amber the moment you run past the original
  • SCORETiming, pacing and delivery marked on every take, or switched off
  • EXPORTThe whole scene as a video, ready to send
Free

Reverse Singing

Record a song and Dubloon flips it. Sing the backwards version you hear, and the app flips your attempt back. Get it right and you come out singing the real song.

  • FLIPBig simple buttons, or the full waveform with loop, speed and pitch
  • SCOREA score for how close you got
  • KEEPSave your best takes and come back to them
Booth Cam · Front camera

Film yourself in the booth.

Switch on the front camera and Dubloon films you while you perform. Export the finished scene with your face in the corner, stacked for a story, or side by side. The camera stays off until you turn it on.
