Quick answers

What is free, and what does Dubloon Pro unlock?

Reverse Singing and Home Video Dub are free. Dubloon Pro unlocks Movie Scene Dub, Sound Imitation, Booth Cam, video exports and community dub packs.

I bought Pro but it is not showing.

Open the Dubloon Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Pro is a universal purchase, so it also unlocks on your Mac.

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on iPhone, open Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds also go through Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue them ourselves.

Dubloon cannot hear me.

Allow the microphone for Dubloon in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone. On a Mac, use System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone.

How do I import a dub pack?

Open the pack from Files, AirDrop or Finder and choose Dubloon, or use Import in the dub library. The dub pack guide has the details.

What happens to my recordings?

They stay on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for exactly what Dubloon collects, and the Terms of Use.
